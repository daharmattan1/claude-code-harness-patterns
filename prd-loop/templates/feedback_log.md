# Plan scorecard log

One row per completed plan, appended when it finishes. When a category has three or more
rows, compare them to its exemplar card and update the card's parameters and failure modes.

Scores are 1 to 5. Criteria fit: did the criteria define done? Sizing: actual iterations
against the estimate. Gate catch: did a gate stop something real? Learning density: reusable
lessons per phase. Rework: phases redone after being marked complete (5 = none).
